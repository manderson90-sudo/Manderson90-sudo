05 / LEADERSHIP

How I lead.

I believe effective leadership starts with understanding people, setting clear expectations and making decisions that are right for the business.

01

People First

I believe in building trust, being approachable and creating an environment where people feel supported and confident in their work. I set clear expectations, encourage personal development and give people the autonomy to take ownership of their responsibilities.

02

Commercially Minded

I take time to understand the wider business, its priorities and the financial implications of decisions. I’m comfortable challenging costs, questioning existing arrangements and making sure technology investments provide value for money.

03

Building Relationships

I place a lot of importance on building strong working relationships with colleagues, stakeholders and suppliers. I value open communication, different perspectives and involving the right people when making decisions or addressing challenges.

04

Focused on Outcomes

I like to establish clear priorities, understand what success looks like and make sure there’s accountability for delivering it. I take a practical approach to solving problems, making improvements and following through on commitments.
